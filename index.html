Operating principle

Capital should enter development after the business case is clear.

New ventures begin with the opportunity, customer, revenue logic and product structure. Existing businesses begin with the workflow, bottlenecks and operational value the system must create.

The result is a digital product designed around a commercial reason, not software built for its own sake.
