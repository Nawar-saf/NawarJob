My role

I work at the decision layer before money gets wasted.

My job is not to sell you a website, an app or marketing before the project itself makes sense. I start with the business decision: what to build, why it fits, what the first version should be and what resources it actually needs.

When execution requires a specialist or licensed provider, the work can be routed separately instead of forcing every project into the same service.
