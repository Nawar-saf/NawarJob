The principle

Do not build software just because you can.

A successful digital project starts with a business reason. Before development, I define the problem, user, revenue model, operating logic and the smallest version that can prove the opportunity.

For an existing business, the objective is different: remove friction, centralize operations and build a system around how the business actually works.
